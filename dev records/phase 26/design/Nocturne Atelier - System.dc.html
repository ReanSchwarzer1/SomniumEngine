01 / IDENTITY

Principles and motifs

Viewport first

Chrome above the scene is a budget, not a canvas. 100 logical px at 1080p; everything else earns its place against the render.

Accent is punctuation

Lunar indigo marks focus, selection, active mode, primary action and links. A panel is never indigo.

Seams, not cards

Editor regions meet at hairlines and go edge to edge. Radius belongs to things you can pick up: inputs, popups, tiles.

One state grammar

Hover wash, 1 px focus ring, 2 px selection rail, dot for modified. The same four cues in the Outliner, Details, Content and log.

Numbers are the product

Tabular figures, right-aligned values, one shared label column. A property table that jitters as you scrub is a broken instrument.

Motion explains space

Drawers travel, popups fade, progress moves. Editing feedback is instant, always.

Permitted motifs
Crescent and lune geometry · the 13° chamfer as a repeated cut · point symmetry · hairline seams · one-pixel indigo rings · monospace numerics · warm sand reserved exclusively for folders
Forbidden
Enclosing rings or shields around the mark · gradients and bevels on chrome · glow · rounded-card panels · a second accent hue · icon-only controls without tooltips · colour as the sole carrier of state · any Lumina or Unreal pixel, hex, mark or asset
02 / MARK

Three routes for the S

All three are point-symmetric about the optical centre and built from the same 64-unit grid.
A · ECLIPSE RECOMMENDED
Two counter-rotating blades on tangent circles, separated by a diagonal channel. The negative space is the S; neither blade is a letterform on its own. Terminals are butt-cut perpendicular to the tangent, which produces the chamfer for free.
B · HORIZONRECOMMENDED
The same skeleton faceted into five straight ribbons per blade. Flatter, more instrument than emblem. Rasterises hardest of the three at 16 px because the facets fall between pixel centres.
C · SIGILRECOMMENDED
True lunes — offset inner circles give a 14.2 → 5.8 unit stroke modulation, so each blade tapers like a crescent. The most expressive and the most fragile: the thin terminals need a hand-corrected micro cut.

Somnium ENGINE
Clear space equals the blade stroke (9.2 units, 0.144 × mark height) on all four sides. Minimum mark size 16 px screen, 6 mm print. Never re-space or re-set the wordmark; convert it to outlines before release.
Similarity review
Enclosing ring / shield
Unreal's silhouette is a circle first. None of the three routes has an outer container — the silhouette is the letter itself.
Blackletter L
Lumina's mark is a calligraphic single stroke with a swash. Ours is constructed from circles and has no calligraphic axis.
Unity / Godot
Both are pictorial (cube, robot head). No letterform collision.
Generic tech S
The risk is real and route A mitigates it with the diagonal channel — the two blades never join, which no type-derived S does. Owner-led trademark clearance is still required.

Details column grammar
Label column is 46 % of panel width, clamped 96–176 px. Value column takes the rest minus the 14 px gutter. Vector rows split the value column into three equal cells with a 4 px gap; the X/Y/Z tag sits inside the field at 10.5 px, text.muted. Labels never wrap — they ellipsise and gain a tooltip. At panel widths under 240 px the row stacks label above value and grows to 40 px.
